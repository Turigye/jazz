How it works

Hold. Speak. Release.

Try it here — press and hold the button, or hold Space. This is a simulation of the real thing, at the speed the real thing runs.

Any app you're in
Put the cursor anywhere and start talking.

It runs where you are

No window to switch to, no box to paste out of. Jazz types into whatever already has your cursor, so dictation fits the way you were working.

Five minutes at a stretch

Long enough for a whole thought. A minute of speech comes back in about five seconds on an M-series Mac.

It knows your words

Add names, jargon and abbreviations it should get right, and short snippets it should expand.

Privacy

There is nowhere to send it.

Most dictation tools upload your microphone to a server. Jazz has no server, no account and no API key — the model sits on your disk and runs on your GPU.

Works on a plane

Turn the network off entirely and nothing changes. Transcription never needed it in the first place.

No account, ever

Download it and use it. There is no sign-up, no licence key and no subscription to forget about.

Open to inspection

The source is public. If you want to know exactly what it does with your audio, you can go and read it.

The meter

The needle is not a bar chart.

A real VU meter deflects linearly with voltage, not decibels — which is why the numbers on its face bunch up at the quiet end. The needle has mass, so it overshoots a transient by a few percent and settles back.

Jazz models both: a damped spring driven by the actual amplitude of your voice. It is the same code moving the needle at the top of this page.
